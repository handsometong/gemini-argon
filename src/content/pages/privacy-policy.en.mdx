## Scope and identity

Gemini 4 Argon Tracker is an independent, unofficial information and research site. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Google. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the site, use its model catalog and tools, subscribe to updates, or access administrator features.

The site is primarily public. You do not need an account to browse model pages, comparison pages, the update log, the RSS feed, or the cost calculator.

## Information we may process

Depending on how you use the site, we may process:

- **Subscription information:** your email address, selected locale, subscription status, confirmation and unsubscribe token hashes, token expiry, confirmation/unsubscribe timestamps, delivery timestamps, and creation/update timestamps.
- **Administrator or registered-user information:** account identifiers, email address, authentication records, sessions, roles and security events where an account is used to maintain the tracker or access protected features.
- **Request and device information:** IP address, browser and user-agent information, approximate request timing, referrer, and security or rate-limiting metadata. Cloudflare may process network and request data as part of hosting, delivery, abuse prevention and security.
- **Usage and analytics information:** page views, referral information, device/browser information and interaction events if analytics is enabled in the current deployment. The site may use a configured analytics provider, such as Plausible, and the provider's own privacy policy also applies.
- **Messages you send us:** information included when you contact the site through a channel published on the website.
- **Public-source information:** model, pricing, benchmark, availability and timeline information collected from public sources. This information is about providers and models, not a profile of you.

We do not ask for payment card details for the public tracker. If a future feature links to a third-party service, that service will process information under its own terms and privacy notice.

## How we use information

We may use information to:

- operate, secure and improve the tracker, its APIs, RSS feed and public pages;
- provide double opt-in update subscriptions and send requested tracker notifications;
- process confirmations and unsubscribes;
- prevent spam, abuse, unauthorized access and excessive requests;
- maintain administrator access and audit changes to source-aware content;
- understand aggregate site usage when analytics is enabled;
- respond to questions and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use subscription emails to sell personal data, and we do not sell personal information.

## Email subscriptions

Subscriptions use double opt-in. After you submit an email address, the service may create a `pending` record and send a confirmation message. The subscription becomes `confirmed` only after the confirmation link is used. Update emails include an unsubscribe link.

Confirmation and unsubscribe tokens are stored as hashes rather than plaintext tokens and are designed to expire. Repeated subscription requests should not reveal whether an email address is already subscribed. If email delivery is not configured or is unavailable, the site may direct you to the RSS feed instead of representing that an email was sent.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in an update email or by using the unsubscribe flow provided by the site.

## Retention and deletion

We retain subscription records while needed to provide the subscription, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or maintain suppression records so that an unsubscribed address is not accidentally re-added. Pending tokens are retained only for their expiration period or until replaced. Administrator and security records may be retained for operational and audit purposes.

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information by using the contact information published on the site. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for security, fraud prevention, or suppression purposes.

## International processing
